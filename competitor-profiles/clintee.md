## Competitive Implications for Roashetta

**Where they're strong vs. us**: Cloud multi-branch sync, patient-facing website/portal, regulatory integrations (e-invoice, Fawry) Roashetta lacks. SaaS = zero-install onboarding.

**Where we're strong vs. them**: Roashetta is **offline desktop** (local SQLite, no internet, data stays in-clinic) — answers data-sovereignty/connectivity concerns a cloud tool can't. Handwritten **drawing-canvas prescription pad** (Egyptian style) + radiology/medical-history canvases are unique. One-time exe + license vs recurring subscription.

**Opportunities**: Attack the "Egyptian company priced in EGP, runs without internet, your patient data never leaves the clinic" angle vs their India HQ / INR / cloud.

**Threats**: If they localize billing to EGP and keep undercutting on price, they win cost-sensitive solo clinics.
