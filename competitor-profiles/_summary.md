## Landscape overview

The Egyptian clinic-management space is dominated by **cloud SaaS subscriptions**, all bilingual AR/EN, all targeting solo→multi-branch clinics. They differentiate on three axes: **local compliance** (Clintee), **modern/AI + interoperability** (ClinicGateway), and **incumbent brand + native EGP pricing** (Remote Clinic). None of the three profiled is offline/desktop-first — which is exactly Roashetta's structural gap in the market.

## Key takeaways

1. **All-cloud field → offline is open.** Every competitor requires internet + recurring subscription. Roashetta's local-SQLite, no-internet, data-stays-in-clinic model is genuinely differentiated — lead with it.
2. **Price spread is wide.** EGP ~450/mo (Clintee) to EGP 3,999/mo (Remote Clinic). A one-time license beats Remote Clinic's ~EGP 48k/yr decisively for single clinics; Clintee is the price threat.
3. **"Built for Egypt" is contestable.** Clintee bills in INR from India; ClinicGateway hides HQ. Roashetta as an actual Egyptian product with EGP pricing has a credibility edge.
4. **Compliance + integrations are the table-stakes gap.** Clintee's MoH reporting, e-invoice, Fawry, national-ID are real Roashetta gaps. These, not features, are likely the deal-breakers for clinics.
5. **No one publishes named customers or strong proof.** Social proof is weak across the board — easy bar to clear with even a few named Egyptian clinics.

---

## Gaps & opportunities for Roashetta

- **Offline/data-sovereignty positioning** — uncontested. "Your patient data never leaves the clinic."
- **One-time license vs subscription fatigue** — strong wedge vs EGP 3,999/mo incumbents.
- **Egyptian prescription drawing canvas** — no competitor offers handwritten-style Rx/radiology pads; it matches real Egyptian doctor workflow.
- **Close the compliance gap** — e-invoice / MoH / Fawry are the biggest catch-up items; without them Roashetta loses clinics that need billing/regulatory.
- **Optional cloud-sync add-on** — competitors' one weakness (offline) is also Roashetta's one weakness (no remote access/multi-branch). An optional sync layer would neutralize their main advantage.
